Visiting the website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools. It does not load fonts or scripts from third parties.

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. When you open a page, Cloudflare technically processes your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the page and protect the site against attacks. This is based on my legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Contact form and email

If you send me a message through the contact form or by email, I use your name, email address, optional phone number and your message only to reply to you and, if we decide to work together, to prepare that work (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Messages from the contact form are delivered to my inbox by the form service Formspree, Inc. (USA), which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

I keep your message as long as needed to answer it and for any follow-up conversation, and delete it when it is no longer needed, unless legal retention periods apply.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what data I hold about you, and ask me to correct, delete or restrict it, or to hand it over to you. You can also object to processing. Just send an email to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).
